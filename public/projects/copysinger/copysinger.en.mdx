## Project Overview

GitHub : [https://github.com/leey00nsu/copy-singer](https://github.com/leey00nsu/copy-singer)
Demo : [https://copysinger.leey00nsu.com/](https://copysinger.leey00nsu.com/)

I have long been interested in TTS and voice cloning and wanted to build a service using them.

The approaches I explored required training a separate model for each user, with GPU costs and preparation time. Using the pretrained SoulX-Singer model, I turned singing voice conversion from a short reference recording into a product without additional per-user training.

I expanded the service beyond voice conversion to analyze the user’s voice, recommend suitable songs and keys, and create an AI mix of the selected song.

## Key Features

- **Voice analysis**: Analyzes observed range, core range, center pitch, and pitch contours from recorded audio
- **Song and key recommendations**: Compares vocal profiles with song ranges to provide fit scores, recommended keys, and reasons
- **AI mixing**: Separates vocals from a selected song, converts them to the user’s voice, and recombines them with the accompaniment

## Key Challenges and Solutions

### Per-user voice cloning training costs

The approaches I explored required training separate model weights for each user, with GPU costs and preparation time. I used the pretrained SoulX-Singer model’s zero-shot approach to generate AI vocals from a short reference recording without additional per-user training.

### Song and key recommendations beyond simple range comparisons

Lowest and highest notes alone do not establish a song’s fit or the key adjustment it needs. I extracted vocal profiles with librosa/pYIN and calculated range overlap, high- and low-note burden, and transposition cost to provide recommendation scores, keys, and reasons together.

### Handling long-running AI jobs

Running lengthy tasks such as analysis, vocal separation, and AI mixing within a web request can leave jobs lost when users leave the page or the server restarts. I used Modal CPU/GPU and a PostgreSQL job queue to process jobs in the background and let users retrieve their status and results later.
