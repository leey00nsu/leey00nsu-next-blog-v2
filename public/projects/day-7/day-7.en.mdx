## Project Overview

GitHub: [https://github.com/leey00nsu/day-7](https://github.com/leey00nsu/day-7)<br />
Demo: [https://day7.leey00nsu.com/](https://day7.leey00nsu.com/)

An **AI-video interactive office drama** that follows an intern during the final week before a full-time employment decision. The story runs from an unsettling assignment on Monday to the final interview on Friday, combining video, timed dialogue captions, narration, and music.

The game remembers every decision made from Monday through Thursday. Those accumulated choices shape one of three endings. Rather than simply playing videos in order, the project connects **choice, memory, media playback, and ending collection into one game flow**.

## Key Features

- **A branching drama that remembers choices**: AI video, captions, and narration tell the story, while four decisions lead to feedback, branching videos, and one of three endings.
- **Scene transitions adapted to playback conditions**: Downloaded videos alternate between two playback layers, with readiness checks for both video and narration. Mobile devices use downloaded playback, while supported environments also offer streaming.
- **Ending collection and choice reports**: Unlocked endings and recent choices are stored in the browser, while choice and ending events are accumulated on the server to show their overall distribution.

## Problem

- **Choices and endings**: Choices accumulated over four days needed to drive scene feedback, branching videos, and the final ending using the same rules.
- **Media transitions**: Video and narration become ready at different times, and network and browser differences can cause delays between scenes or interrupt playback.
- **Progress and records**: Starting a new game needed to reset progress while retaining preferences and collected endings. Aggregate choice statistics also needed to remain separate from personal records.

## Solution

- **Choices and endings**: Scenes and choices were separated into content data, while progress was handled by state transition functions. Choices accumulate during each playthrough, and the number of choices of a given type determines one of three endings.
- **Media transitions**: Download mode alternates between two video layers and checks video and narration readiness before playback. Mobile devices require media downloads, while supported environments offer streaming as an alternative.
- **Progress and records**: Progress lives in a page-lifetime Zustand store, while preferences, endings, and recent choices are stored in localStorage. The server stores and aggregates choice and ending events alongside an anonymous identifier.

## Impact

- **Choices and endings**: The game connects immediate reactions to the final outcome through a single choice history.
- **Media transitions**: Playback behavior adapts to the browser environment, with controls that prevent the next scene from playing before its media is ready.
- **Progress and records**: Each new playthrough starts from the beginning while keeping previous preferences and unlocked endings. Players can view their own records and the overall choice distribution separately.
