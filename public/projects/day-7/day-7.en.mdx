## Project Overview

GitHub: [https://github.com/leey00nsu/day-7](https://github.com/leey00nsu/day-7)<br />
Demo: [https://day7.leey00nsu.com/](https://day7.leey00nsu.com/)

An **AI-video interactive office drama** that follows an intern during the final week before a full-time employment decision. The story runs from an unsettling assignment on Monday to the final interview on Friday, combining video, timed dialogue captions, narration, and music.

The game remembers every decision made from Monday through Thursday. Those accumulated choices shape one of three endings. Rather than simply playing videos in order, the project connects **choice, memory, media playback, and ending collection into one game flow**.

## Key Features

- Seamless scene transitions using alternating video layers
- JSON-based speakers, dialogue captions, timecodes, and chapter narration
- Choice feedback and three endings determined by accumulated decisions
- An ending album that stores unlocked endings and recent choices
- Controls for master volume, music, sound effects, and caption size
- First-visit media preloading with a network streaming fallback
- PostgreSQL reports that accumulate choices and endings per playthrough
- Letterboxed video that preserves the full frame on mobile screens

## Problem

- **Gaps between videos**: Switching sources between multiple scenes can expose loading states or black frames.
- **Consistent choice state**: Decisions, feedback, and ending evaluation must use the same state across several screens and four in-story days.
- **Reliable mobile playback**: Network and browser differences make it difficult to guarantee smooth transitions with streaming alone.
- **Different audio lifecycles**: Dialogue, narration, music, and sound effects must stay synchronized with scene changes and player preferences.
- **Play history persistence**: Browser-side ending progress and server-side playthrough reports need different storage lifecycles.

## Solution

- **Alternating video layers**: Prepare the current and next scenes on two video layers and swap them to reduce visible gaps during transitions.
- **Separate content from state transitions**: Keep scene videos, choices, and feedback in content data while implementing choice accumulation and ending evaluation as pure state rules.
- **Media manifest and playback fallback**: With player consent, preload about 36 MB of video and audio as session Blob URLs; fall back to streaming when preparation is declined or fails.
- **Scoped Zustand stores**: Persist volume and caption preferences while keeping one playthrough's story progress in a page-lifetime store.
- **Separate client and server records**: Store unlocked and recent endings in `localStorage`, while accumulating every playthrough's choices and ending in PostgreSQL.
- **Responsive video stage**: Preserve the entire video frame with letterboxing on mobile and allow settings to change during playback.

## Impact

- **Continuous story experience**: Video, captions, narration, and music flow from the prologue through Friday's final interview as a cohesive scene.
- **Visible consequences**: Four accumulated choices lead to three endings and an ending album, encouraging repeat playthroughs.
- **Playback across environments**: Preloading and streaming fallback support different desktop and mobile network conditions.
- **Maintainable content model**: Separating story data, state transitions, and media control makes scenes and choices easier to update.
- **Playthrough analytics foundation**: Anonymous browser identifiers allow repeated choices and endings to be accumulated for later analysis.
